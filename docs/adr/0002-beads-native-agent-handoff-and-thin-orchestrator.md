# Beads-native Agent handoff and thin Orchestrator

Instead of sandboxed JSON workers, local state file persistence, or `bd formula` sub-bead molecules, Nightweaver keeps workflow state on the **Product Bead** and **Plan Bead** in `bd` labels and comments. **Agents** (`Planner`, `Plan Challenger`, `Implementer`, `Reviewer`, `QA`, `Merger`) execute `bd`, `git`, and `gh` operations within their role boundaries and supply findings for Orchestrator verification.

Before live startup recovery or dispatch writes, the **Orchestrator** validates the Target Repository read-only and acquires an OS-managed process-lifetime mutex keyed to the physical Git common directory. The mutex applies across Project names and Nightweaver host roots, stays outside the Target Repository, and releases when its owning process exits or dies; recovery uses no lease database, heartbeat, PID file, or proof-of-death service. Under exclusion, startup releases only claims still `in_progress` and assigned exactly to the current Project's recovery actor, using status and assignee guards and fresh reads around uncertain writes. It preserves stage, pause labels, repair counts, and all other Bead fields, then reads actual current inventory and native readiness before admission. Foreign and other-Project claims remain untouched. Required tracker failures and unusable shared runtime roots stop startup. `--dry-run` inspects the same proposed recovery without acquiring recovery authority. Startup recovery decisions do not authorize Agents to adopt or release claims. Comments, notes, description edits, and audit history supply context only; they never authorize execution.

The selector-only CLI connects recovered state to continuous Delivery and singleton Merger pools. Startup, best-effort journal hints from the configured Target Repository, an always-on 30-second timer, and completions from either pool after local capacity release share one coalesced, serialized discovery path. Each pass reads fresh eligibility and evaluates Delivery before Merger. Both pools reserve their own bounded capacity and queue preparation without waiting for an Agent attempt, so slow preparation in one pool leaves the other available. Failed tracker reads admit no stale work and wait for a later trigger. Journal setup and attachment do not gate startup or require historical replay. Tail termination or truncation requests discovery and reconnects with capped exponential backoff while timer and both completion streams remain active. The singleton Merger reuses the verified rejection publisher and completion finalizer: the Orchestrator checks current-invocation evidence, publishes any guarded repair handoff, and closes only after deterministic verification. Automatic Planning remains outside the current runtime.

The first `Ctrl+C` closes new reservations and native Agent-launch boundaries before stopping discovery. Preparation already in flight settles without launching; an already launched turn can complete verification, bounded outcome classification including its permitted fallback, and guarded result recording under its ordinary timeout. Shutdown starts no next role, operational retry, duplicate-failure classifier, or retry-only reset. It rereads ownership before guarded claim finalization, retains unfinished and paused work, and drains every slot without a fixed two-minute cutoff. A failed turn or required finalization/read failure makes shutdown unsuccessful after the other slots drain. A second signal atomically closes all active Agent Job Objects and exits unsuccessfully without awaiting executor cleanup, classification, Git/Beads operations, verification, resets, environment cleanup, or claim release. It starts no fallback or compensating writes, later signals do not extend shutdown, and the repository mutex remains held through process exit. Restart rereads actual tracker/filesystem state and performs ordinary guarded recovery while retaining unfinished and paused work.

Live startup reconciles completed-environment obligations after exact-claim recovery and before fresh admission reads, without removing resources. One serialized cleanup worker consumes coalesced completion, journal and timer hints in bounded batches outside admission and Delivery/Merger capacity, sharing per-Bead lifetime reservations with both pools. The **Orchestrator** removes only a `planned` Product Bead's detached Planning worktree or a closed Plan Bead's exact Delivery branch checkout after fresh Bead, registration, canonical-path, ownership, lock, linkage, and path-safety checks. A new pause or changed checkout identity cancels a stale candidate. Locked, missing/prunable, unregistered, linked, deleted-Bead, and ambiguous leftovers remain and are diagnosed. Cleanup uses targeted `git worktree remove --force`; it never runs startup-wide `git worktree prune` or deletes branches by name. Tmp deletion follows observed surviving worktrees and directories and applies only to known completed Beads. The shared completed-environment cleanup module atomically persists versioned Project/root/repository-bound obligations before removal and records per-resource progress and verified absence. Known transient sharing/busy failures persist an initial attempt plus at most three automatic retries after 30 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes across restart. Policy/access denial, unsafe identity, uncertain process settlement and exhaustion require independent cleanup attention. A completion that becomes missing, paused, reopened or assigned is durably retained for attention and dequeued without blocking unrelated obligations or graceful drain. Failed in-flight observations settle to attention under serialization after owned operations return; cancellation retains pending evidence for settlement on the next wake or restart, never blind removal replay. Required persistence failure remains explicit and the next wake first retries interrupted-attempt settlement. Total/per-cycle attempts and prior failure/denial evidence survive guarded operator Retry through the versioned live-owner/revision-bound atomic inbox. Local failures protect remaining resources and allow unrelated cleanup to proceed. They do not write pause labels or change successful Delivery completion, claims, stages, or repair budgets. Existing pauses remain retention boundaries. An unavailable or invalid obligation store, required tracker failure, or unusable shared root stops cleanup/startup; successful prior cleanup is not rolled back. Exact verified branch deletion remains a separate one-shot Merger completion responsibility and can proceed after confirmed worktree absence despite tmp-only failure; startup, timer and operator retries never add branch operations. Graceful shutdown stops new removals, settles or cancels cleanup and flushes completion evidence after pool drain. Force stop starts no compensating cleanup; dry-run makes no logical cleanup writes or removals.

Human pause is an environment-retention boundary: keep the local worktree, current branch and `HEAD`, all tracked and untracked files, ignored caches, logs, and Bead temporary files so a person can inspect or resume the work; release only the claim still owned by Nightweaver. A human resumes paused work through the Attention Desk's explicit Resume or Retry action. The request carries the displayed stage, full pause-label set, and repair count; the host compares them with fresh state before writing an audit comment. One native update guarded for open status and an empty assignee removes both pause labels and unsets `repair_cycles`, preserving the current stage and unrelated fields. Abort closes without clearing pause state or repair budget. If the state update fails or its result is uncertain, current tracker reads determine the reported outcome; an audit comment is never treated as a command. Only a successful persisted Resume or Retry makes the Bead eligible for ordinary dependency, capacity, stage, and claim scheduling. The action does not directly launch an Agent, and fresh discovery after restart uses the same rules. A validated resume reuses the retained checkout without an implicit fetch or reset. Lifecycle authority belongs to the Orchestrator for every Delivery role, not only Implementer.

Implementer, Reviewer, QA, and Merger are evidence-producing workers, not workflow-state owners. Implementer may edit, commit, push, and create or update its pull request. Reviewer and QA perform their assigned review or verification work. Merger synchronizes the branch, runs repository checks, and supplies merge or rejection evidence. Each records findings, checks, and any concrete product question in the existing invocation log or Bead comments. None changes status, type, stage or pause labels, assignee, or `repair_cycles`. After each process exits, the Orchestrator collects bounded evidence with source IDs, classifies it using role-specific allowed outcomes, independently checks process/worktree/Git and tracker state, and publishes the verified result under current status and assignee guards. Confirmed cross-pool handoff receipts end outgoing tracker and environment operations; local capacity release and discovery notification follow.

Delivery outcome classification uses one OpenAI Decisions choice request as primary and at most one configured standard Agent CLI classifier fallback with the same question, choices, and evidence. Implementer outcomes are `ready_for_review`, `product_decision_required`, `technical_blocker`, and `incomplete_or_unclear`; Reviewer and QA outcomes are `approved`, `changes_required`, `product_decision_required`, `technical_blocker`, and `incomplete_or_unclear`. Missing credentials or unavailable or invalid primary responses invoke the fallback; a valid unclear or non-approval answer stops immediately. Invalid or unavailable fallback, failed independent verification, or a technical blocker preserves the work and evidence, leaves the input stage and repair counter unchanged, and escalates to `needs-human` without an operational retry or another Delivery role run. A verified approval advances to the next stage; a verified repair finding returns to `stage:implement` with the shared repair count incremented in the same guarded update. A verified concrete product question publishes its pause state and releases the claim in one guarded update. QA approval publishes `stage:merge`, opens the Bead, and clears the assignee atomically. The receipt is accepted from the structured update result; if that result is ambiguous, one fresh read may confirm it. After confirmed handoff, the Delivery slot performs no more Bead or worktree operations. This outcome classifier is separate from the existing duplicate operational failure classifier. No classification queue, decision ledger, replay marker, or new workflow stage is introduced.

Merger local-check verification separates log volume from evidence completeness. A dedicated reader scans a canonical settled invocation log within an 8 MiB work bound, detects duplicate or quoted frame markers across the whole scanned log, and consumes at most 32 KiB of complete, strictly decoded UTF-8 evidence. The comment and frame must agree on command, checked HEAD, positive exit code, summary, and diagnostic, with terminal failure totals. Unrelated output outside the frame does not establish the check result; the producer includes the final check outcome, including a later passing rerun, inside the frame. Oversized logs or incomplete evidence retain the established diagnostic pause and environment. The duplicate operational failure reader and all workflow, ownership, PR, and repair-publication guards remain unchanged.
